  The project cleaned and organized historical records of the wars of the Spring and Autumn and Warring States periods — normalizing fields such as date, state, belligerents and outcome — then built an interactive visualization with D3.js, supporting filtering by time, state and battle type. The interface was designed in a historical style, and the result was covered by **The Paper**.

* **项目类型**：数字人文 · 数据可视化
* **时间**：2022.07 – 2022.08
* **成果**：北京大学可视化暑期学校**优秀课程设计**

项目清洗、整理春秋战国时期的战争史料，统一时间、国别、参战方与胜负等字段，再用 D3.js 做成可交互的可视化，支持按时间、国家与战役类型筛选。界面按历史风格设计，成果被**澎湃新闻**报道。
