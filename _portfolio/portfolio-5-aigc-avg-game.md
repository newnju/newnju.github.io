  * **Type**: AIGC · Interactive narrative / Hackathon
  * **Period**: May 2023
  * **Outcome**: **9th place** at the Peking University New Engineering Hackathon

  **ChatGPT** drives the story, generating subsequent plots according to the player's choices; once the plot advances, it is asked to write prompts recognizable by **Stable Diffusion** from the scene descriptions. The prompts are fed to Stable Diffusion to generate images, which are combined with the text plot into a complete AVG experience.

* **项目类型**：AIGC · 交互叙事 / 黑客马拉松
* **时间**：2023.05
* **成果**：北京大学新工科黑客马拉松**第 9 名**

以 **ChatGPT** 作为故事的推进者，根据玩家选择生成后续剧情；剧情推进后，再让它依据场景描述生成 **Stable-Diffusion** 可识别的 prompt；prompt 传入 Stable-Diffusion 出图，与文本剧情合成完整的 AVG 体验。
