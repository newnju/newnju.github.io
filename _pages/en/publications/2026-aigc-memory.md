excerpt: Generative AI is shifting historical image production from scholarly verification to probabilistic generation. Taking Tang-dynasty imagery of multi-ethnic interaction as its case, this paper runs prompt perturbation and coding analysis on 162 images from six text-to-image models, argues that the incommensurability between probabilistic optimization and historical verification is conditional, and proposes a resilience-oriented governance framework that prioritizes making uncertainty explicit.
