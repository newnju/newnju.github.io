I am **Jiawen Wu (武嘉文)**, a Ph.D. student in Journalism and Communication at Nanjing University's School of Journalism and Communication, specialising in visual history and the historical imagery of the Chinese nation. I received my M.A. in Art History from the School of Arts, Nanjing University, and my B.A. in Broadcasting and Television Directing from the School of Journalism and Communication, Nanjing Xiaozhuang University; from October 2023 to March 2024 I studied on exchange at Eberhard Karls Universität Tübingen in Germany. From July to August 2019, I received the Jiangsu Government Scholarship for Overseas Study and studied digital media research at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA).
