* **Type**: Jiangsu Provincial Undergraduate Innovation Training Program (Project No. 201911460038Y)
* **Role**: Project leader
* **Period**: Sep 2019 – Sep 2021
* **Outcome**: **Outstanding completion** of the Jiangsu Provincial Undergraduate Innovation Training Program

Nanjing Baiju is a form of narrative singing popular in Nanjing and surrounding areas, and is national intangible cultural heritage. The project promoted Baiju through short videos, social media and other channels, reaching an audience of more than 10,000. It also planned and directed the documentary *Huang Lingling's Baiju Life*, handling everything from script writing and shoot organization to post-production. In addition, I coordinated resources both on and off campus, including inheritors of the intangible cultural heritage and the film crew.
