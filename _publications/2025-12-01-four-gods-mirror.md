body_en: |-
  This paper discusses the naming and dating of Han-dynasty TLV (boju) mirrors. On the older name “guju (rules-and-square) mirror”, Zhou Zheng — working from the inscription “刻具博局去不羊” on a rubbing of a mirror in the National Museum of China — argued that it should be renamed “boju mirror” (TLV mirror), and this view has since been accepted by the field. Judging from excavated and transmitted materials, TLV mirrors belong mainly to the late Western Han, Xin Mang and early Eastern Han periods, of which the four-deity TLV mirrors with inscriptions beginning “Shangfang” are the most typical.

  The article takes a four-deity TLV mirror of the Xin Mang period held by the National Museum of China as its example. The mirror is 23 cm in diameter (one chi in the Xin Mang period was 23.1 cm), with a rim 0.5 cm thick, a round knob and a four-petal knob base. Within a double-line square frame, twelve nipples on round bases alternate with the twelve earthly branches in seal script; outside the frame, eight inward-facing arc-patterned nipples and the TLV layout divide the inner field into four quarters and eight poles, occupied by the Azure Dragon, Vermilion Bird, White Tiger and Black Tortoise. The outer field is a 64-character inscription band in clerical script; the inner edge of the broad flat rim carries a saw-tooth pattern and the outer edge a cloud-and-qi pattern.

  On this basis, the paper proposes dividing the ornament of the four-deity TLV mirror into three parts: the rim, the outer field and the inner field. This division differs both from splitting inner and outer fields at the TLV pattern, and from assigning the rim inscription band to the outer field. Finally, drawing on the mirror's circular form, the square frame at the center of the TLV pattern, and related passages in the *Jinshu · Tianwen zhi*, *Zhoubi suanjing* and *Dadai Liji*, the article discusses how the cosmological notion of “round heaven and square earth” is expressed in the ornament on the mirror's back.

本文讨论汉代博局镜的定名与流行年代。关于“规矩镜”这一旧称，周铮据中国历史博物馆藏铜镜拓本上的“刻具博局去不羊”铭文，主张改称“博局镜”，这一看法目前已为学界接受。从出土与传世材料看，博局镜主要属西汉后期、新莽与东汉前期，其中铭文以“尚方”开头的四神博局镜最为典型。

文章以中国国家博物馆藏的一面新莽四神博局镜为例。此镜直径 23 厘米（新莽一尺为 23.1 厘米），缘厚 0.5 厘米，圆钮，四叶纹钮座，双线方框内十二圆座乳钉与十二辰铭相间，字体为篆书；方框外八枚内向连弧纹座乳钉与 TLV 博局纹将内区划分为四方八极，分置青龙、朱雀、白虎、玄武；外区为六十四字隶书铭文带，宽平缘内圈饰锯齿纹、外圈饰云气纹。

在此基础上，本文提出把四神博局镜纹饰分为镜边、外区、内区三部分。这一分法既不同于以博局纹为界划分内外两区，也不同于把镜边铭文带归入外区的做法。文章最后结合博局镜的圆形形制、博局纹中心的方框，以及《晋书·天文志》《周髀算经》《大戴礼记》等文献中的相关记载，讨论“天圆地方”宇宙观在镜背纹饰上的体现。
